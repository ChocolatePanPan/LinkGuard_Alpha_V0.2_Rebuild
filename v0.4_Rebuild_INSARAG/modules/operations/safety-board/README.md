# Safety Board

Safety Board 用於管理人員進出、危害、撤離與安全確認。

## Track

- team check-in
- team check-out
- last contact time
- hazard zones
- evacuation alerts
- missing or overdue team
- PPE and special risk notes

## Rule

安全事件優先級高於一般任務狀態。
# Safety Board

Safety Board 顯示隊伍安全、危害與進出紀錄。

## Track

- team check-in
- team check-out
- missing member
- hazard zones
- collapse risk
- evacuation order
- PPE issue

## Rule

安全事件優先於一般任務，同步佇列也要排在任務狀態前面。
